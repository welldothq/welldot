The Portuguese label of the `permit_document` attachment type is now "Portaria / licença" instead of "Portaria / outorga", matching the "licença" wording used for permits in the profiler and `@welldot/pdf`. The `Permit` TSDoc now describes a permit as a legal instrument that authorizes, exempts or registers the well or its use (drilling, abstraction, dewatering), not only abstraction, as the existing `drilling_permit` type already allowed.
