The format's changelog is part of the specification: each revision adds a "Changes in v2.x" section to the [overview](/format/spec/v2/overview). This page lists the revisions and links to those sections, so there is one record of what changed. Package releases have their own changelogs ([core](/core/changelog), [utils](/utils/changelog), [render](/render/changelog)).

## In brief

- **2.4** writes the version as the string `"M.m"` and adds the site time zone, data logger series, offline `.wellz` packages and vendor-named extensions, plus clarifications. Readers older than `@welldot/core` 0.5.0 reject `"2.4"` files. No deprecations ([details](/format/spec/v2/overview#changes-in-v24)).
- **2.3** adds the operational blocks (attachments, pumps, permits, meters, production, operating regime) and water quality samples, with recommended vocabularies for most open fields. No deprecations ([details](/format/spec/v2/overview#changes-in-v23)).
- **2.1** adds `well_purpose` and `centralizers`, and deprecates `well_type: "artesian"` ([details](/format/spec/v2/overview#changes-in-v21), [deprecations](/format/spec/v2/overview#deprecations-1)).
- **2.0** adds `location`, `well_id`, profiles, the texture object, hydrodynamic events, aquifer analysis, history logs and precision fields, and fixes all units to SI ([details](/format/spec/v2/overview#changes-from-v1)).

Within version 2, every revision is additive: a valid file from an earlier revision stays valid ([deprecation policy](/format/spec/v2/format-reference#deprecation-policy)). [Versions and deprecation](/format/concepts/versions-and-deprecation) explains how readers handle them.
